Calm the floor so a biome reads as one surface, and remove the dark screen frame

Two complaints, both fixed.

1. The floor looked dizzying once tiles met, and a biome did not read as one piece of land.
   Three separate causes, all in the tile itself:

   * The top face ran an ordered-dither RAMP across every tile - up to 58% density of the lit tone
     at the west edge, falling to the shaded tone at the east. Tiled 312 times that is a sawtooth
     repeating every 96 pixels. It is now a single fixed-density 4x4 Bayer speckle at 16%, which is
     the same everywhere and, because 96 and 48 are both multiples of the 4-pixel period, lines up
     exactly across a tile boundary. One continuous speckled surface, no seam.

   * Every tile carried a 3px bevel on all four edges, which drew a bright and dark diamond outline
     around every single tile - a visible grid of separate objects. The bevels are gone. A floor's
     light direction belongs to the scene, not to each tile: it lives in the two cliff faces (lit
     west, shaded east, and only drawn where the ground actually changes height) and in the ambient
     occlusion under every structure, tree and prop.

   * Every tile painted the identical detail at the identical position, so a field of two hundred
     tiles showed the same grass tuft in a perfect grid. `drawSurface` now takes a variant offset,
     picked by the renderer from the tile's grid position with a pair of multipliers coprime with the
     variant count, so neighbouring tiles scatter differently. Four layouts per biome, which breaks
     the grid at board zoom without quadrupling the sprite cache.

   The grid contour is also fainter (0.55 -> 0.3 alpha), so tiles merge while the board still reads
   as spaces.

   This changes what the light test should assert, and the reasoning is worth recording. Satisfying
   "flat tiles are lit from the left" required exactly the ramp that caused the complaint, so a flat
   tile now has to be NEUTRAL than lit: the assertion is |left - right| < 0.02, which passes with a
   worst case of 0.009. The tile-level light assertion moved to the cliffed tiles, where the light
   actually is, and now requires a 0.02 margin on the whole sprite (worst case 0.040). The existing
   test that the two cliff faces agree with each other is unchanged.

   Two new tests replace the weaker one the ramp needed. The speckle is now measured on the BARE
   ground, via a new `detail: false` option: an earlier version measured the tile with its detail on
   and had to accept a 0.06 density spread, because a grass tuft sitting on four speckle pixels is
   indistinguishable from a speckle that moved - a threshold loose enough to pass almost anything.
   The bare ground is asserted to be exactly two tones, periodic with the tile dimensions, under
   0.16 contrast, and even in density to within 0.05. A second test proves `detail: false` is a real
   switch and not a way to pass against a blank tile.

2. There was a dark frame around the play area. It was the screen vignette this project gained two
   commits ago - first a radial gradient, then hard bands - and either way it reads as a black
   border around the board rather than as atmosphere, which is not what a board game wants around
   the board. Removed outright from the board renderer. The battle cutscene keeps its dimming,
   because that is functional dimming for the duration of a cutscene.

Verification: 133 tests green, typecheck green, 11/11 Tailwind spot checks, 12/12 pixel UI
components present, 8/8 glass utilities absent. The 60 committed floor tiles are regenerated.
